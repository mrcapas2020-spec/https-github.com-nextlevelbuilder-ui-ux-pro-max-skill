## CONFIGURAÇÕES RECOMENDADAS NO GAMMA

Ao colar o prompt:
- **Theme:** Choose "Dark" or "Night" as base theme
- **Accent color:** Set to purple (#8b5cf6)
- **Font:** Inter (or similar geometric sans-serif)
- **Layout density:** Minimal / Spacious
- **Card style:** Glass / Dark cards
